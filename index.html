FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything about certificates, documents, payment, and timelines — in one place.

What is an RVSF?

A Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facility is a government-authorised centre for dismantling end-of-life vehicles. Only an RVSF can issue a Certificate of Deposit.

How is my vehicle's scrap value calculated?

It is based on vehicle type, kerb weight, and current scrap metal rates. The online figure is an estimate. The final value is confirmed after physical verification.

What documents do I need?

Original RC, the owner's ID proof (Aadhaar or PAN), and bank details. If the vehicle is under loan, you need an NOC from the financier. If someone else is handing it over, you need an authorisation letter from the owner.

Will I receive a Certificate of Deposit?

Yes. It is issued digitally once the vehicle is accepted at the facility. It is your legal proof of scrapping and can be used to claim applicable benefits on a new vehicle purchase, subject to your state's rules.

How long does the process take?

Our team calls back within 24 hours. Pickup and certificate timelines vary by location; we confirm exact dates during the call.

When do I get paid?

Payment is transferred to your bank account after verification at the facility. We confirm the timeline once documents are verified.

What if my vehicle doesn't run?

It doesn't need to. We tow it.
